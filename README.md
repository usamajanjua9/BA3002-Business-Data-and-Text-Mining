## 🎯 Course Overview

Business Data and Text Mining introduces students to the methods, tools, and workflows used to discover useful patterns and actionable insights from structured and unstructured business data.

The course begins with business problem formulation and the data mining lifecycle, followed by data collection, preprocessing, exploratory analysis, classification, clustering, association rule mining, and model evaluation.

The second part of the course focuses on text mining and Natural Language Processing, including text preprocessing, Bag of Words, TF-IDF, sentiment analysis, text classification, topic modeling, and introductory modern NLP techniques.

The emphasis throughout the course is on answering one core question:

> **What does the data tell the business, and what should the business do next?**

* Discover topics and patterns from business text

# 🤖 Responsible Use of Generative AI

Generative AI may be used as a **learning assistant**, but students remain responsible for understanding and validating their work.

A simple rule:

> **If you cannot explain the code or result, do not submit it as your own analytical work.**

Students should verify:

* Generated code
* Analytical conclusions
* Statistics
* References
